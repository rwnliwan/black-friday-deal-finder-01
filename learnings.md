# Learnings — black-friday-deal-finder-01 (DealHound)

## 1. Verify the price on the day, not the brief's price: Black Friday prices move within the week

Several prices in the brief had already moved by the time this session checked them (27 Nov): the AirTag 2 had dropped from $23 to $18, the Galaxy S26 Ultra had risen from $950 to $1,049, the Beats Studio Pro was $139.95 rather than $149, and the Bose QuietComfort 2nd Gen was $219 rather than $167. Every one of those would have been a stale or invented price in finalist review if copied from the brief. The fix that worked: re-check each deal against a dated source in the same session and use the newest confirmed figure, noting the movement in the evidence line (e.g. "was $23 earlier in the week") — the movement itself becomes proof the price was really checked.
