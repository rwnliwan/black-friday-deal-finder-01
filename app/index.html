How we verify

  1. Read the price at the store — or in a deals source that names the store and the price. Nothing here comes from a guess or a stale roundup.
  2. Check the maths — the % off is computed from the sale price and the usual price shown on the listing, never from a headline.
  3. Label it honestly — NEW LOW means the lowest price ever tracked on that listing; STRONG means a genuinely big cut; ROUTINE means the sale recurs and the saving is small.
  4. Stamp the time — every card shows when its price was checked and where it was read, so you can re-check it yourself before you buy.

Prices move fast on Black Friday. The store's live price at checkout is the final word — if a price has changed, the link still takes you to the product.
